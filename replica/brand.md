# Brand: Flickpoint

## Name
Shortlist (personal project, so screening checks are **to run** if it ever goes public: USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, App Store search, domain whois, handles):
1. **Flickpoint** ← picked: says the whole interaction (point, flick to click)
2. Budwand
3. Airwand
4. Tiltclick
5. Pointlight

"AirPods" is Apple's trademark: it appears only to say which hardware works, never in the app's name or icon.

## Palette
The demo uses neon green on near-black with a cyan line. Flickpoint moves to a different hue family: a warm coral accent on system materials, adapting to light and dark mode.

| role | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| accent | `#E2502A` | `#FF7A52` |
| accent-soft (meter fill) | accent @ 25% | accent @ 30% |
| ok | system green | system green |
| warn | system orange | system orange |
| surface / text | system (`.regularMaterial`, `.primary`, `.secondary`) | system |

Large rate readout: SF Rounded, semibold, monospaced digits (system font, not licensed).

## Logo brief
Mark: a cursor arrow with two short motion arcs behind it (SF Symbol `cursorarrow.motionlines` stands in for the menu-bar glyph). Must read at 16 px; must not resemble Apple's AirPods glyphs.

## Voice
- **Plain**, not technical: "Aim at the right edge", not "capture +X basis vector".
- **Short**, not curt.
- **Calm**, not cute.

Most-seen strings: "Aim at the center and hold still", "Now aim at the right edge", "Now aim at the top edge", "Ready. Flick to click.", "Paused", "No motion headphones connected", "Recenter", "Calibrate…", "Pointer control".
